Sentiment Visualization

Sentiment Gradient

The customer sentiment ring on the Summary page, and the customer + agent bars on the Sentiment Analysis card, both show a blended color timeline of the conversation instead of a single flat color — so you can see how the mood moved, not just where it ended up.

AI Insights tab→Summary panel · Transcript tab→Sentiment Analysis card

01

What you're looking at

A quick way to spot how a conversation actually went.

Both widgets used to show one flat color sized only by the overall score — a call scored 62% drew a bar 62% full, in one color. That hid everything about how the conversation actually moved: a call that started tense and calmed down looked identical to one that was mildly negative the whole way through.

Now each part of the conversation is placed in speaking order, colored by how that moment scored, and the colors blend smoothly into one another. Reading the ring clockwise, or the bar left to right, shows you the shape of the call — not just its ending score.

Negative (0–40) Neutral (41–60) Positive (61–100)

03

Summary page: customer sentiment score

One ring, always the customer, always on the AI Insights tab.

The circular "Customer Sentiment Score" widget on the Summary panel is built the same way — colored moments blended around the ring in the order they happened. The number in the center is still the customer's overall score; the ring around it is the shape behind that number.

04

Sentiment Analysis: customer & agent

Every speaker gets their own row, and their own blended bar.

The Sentiment Analysis card, on the Transcript tab, lists every participant — customer first, then agent (and anyone else on the call) — each with a score and a bar. Every row's bar is built independently from that speaker's own moments, so one person having a shorter or bumpier conversation doesn't distort the other's bar.

Transcript — Sentiment AnalysisPer speaker
Customer(cust_84a2...) 54%
Agent(agt_priya.k) 81%

A few things this makes easy to spot at a glance:

  • Whether the customer or the agent shifted mood first — compare where each bar's color changes.
  • Any number of speakers on a call (not just two) get their own row and bar, laid out the same way.
  • The percentage next to each bar is that speaker's overall score — the bar next to it is the detail behind that number.

05

When nothing shows up

A blank space means "not scored yet," not an error.

If a speaker's moment-by-moment sentiment hasn't been scored yet, the ring or bar for that speaker simply doesn't appear rather than showing a guess:

  • Sentiment Analysis bars — the bar for that row is left blank; the score percentage next to it still shows whatever's available, or "N/A".
  • Summary ring — the ring stays empty until scoring is available for that record.
