Transcript Generation

Voice Percentage Detection

Before PLAY turns a call or video recording into a transcript, it listens to the file and works out how much of it is real speech. Recordings that are mostly silence, hum or noise are stopped early, so you do not wait for a transcript that would be empty.

Recording List→a call or video record→Transcript→Generate Transcript

01

What this feature does

A quick quality check on the sound, before the transcript.

When you click Generate Transcript on a call or video recording, PLAY first asks: how much of this recording is somebody talking? The answer is a single number, the voice percentage:

  • 100% — people are talking the whole time.
  • 50% — about half of the recording is speech, the rest is pauses or background sound.
  • 0% — no speech was found at all.

If the number is lower than the minimum your organisation has chosen, PLAY tells you and does not generate the transcript for that recording.

The check only reads the recording. Nothing is changed, saved or sent anywhere else, and the recording plays exactly as before.

02

How PLAY measures speech

Two tools, one after the other: FFmpeg prepares the sound, Silero VAD listens to it.

Step 1 — FFmpeg prepares the sound. FFmpeg is a well-known media tool. PLAY uses it to open your recording, whatever format it is in (wav, mp3, mp4 and so on), drop any picture from a video, and convert the sound to one plain channel at 16,000 samples per second. This is the one format the next step understands. The recording on your storage is not touched.

Step 2 — Silero VAD listens. Silero VAD is a small, open-source voice-detection model (VAD stands for voice activity detection). PLAY feeds it the sound in tiny pieces of 32 milliseconds each, about thirty a second. For each piece the model gives a probability between 0 and 1 that someone is speaking. A piece with a probability of 0.5 or more counts as speech; anything lower does not.

Step 3 — PLAY counts. The voice percentage is the number of speech pieces divided by the number of all pieces, shown to two decimals. PLAY also works out the total length and the amount of speech in seconds.

Why a voice model and not just loudness? Line hum, static and background noise can be loud without being speech, and real speech can be quiet. Silero VAD was trained to tell speech from other sound, so a noisy recording with nobody talking scores low, and a quiet recording with real conversation scores high.

03

A worked example

Why a few numbers decide the outcome.

A 10-minute call is 600 seconds, which is 18,750 slices of 32 ms (600 ÷ 0.032). Suppose the minimum is 15%:

04

What you see

From the click to the result.

  1. Open a call or video recording and go to Transcript
    Wait for the recording to load, then click the Generate Transcript button.
  2. PLAY checks the audio
    A loader shows while the voice percentage is measured. This takes a few seconds for a short call and longer for a long recording.
  3. If the speech is enough
    PLAY goes straight on and generates the transcript. You do nothing extra.
  4. If the speech is too low
    A warning appears: Low speech detected (8%, minimum 15%). PLAY then asks for the transcript, is refused, and shows: This recording doesn’t seem to contain enough speech to process (only about 8% detected, out of a required 15%). Please make sure the audio has actual conversation and isn’t mostly silence or background noise, then try again.

Nothing is saved when a recording is stopped here, so you can try again, for example after checking that the right recording is attached.

05

Settings

Chosen once for the whole organisation by your administrator.

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Enable speech ratio detectionTurns the check on or off. When off, nothing is measured and every recording goes straight to the transcript, as before.Off
Minimum speech ratioThe lowest voice percentage that is accepted, from 0 to 100. A recording at or above it passes.15 (%)

If the minimum is left empty, or holds something that is not a number between 0 and 100, PLAY uses 15%. A higher minimum is stricter: setting it to 40 stops recordings that are less than 40% speech.

Ask your administrator. These two settings are part of the tenant configuration (PLAY_TNT_ENABLE_SPEECH_RATIO_DETECTION and PLAY_TNT_MIN_SPEECH_RATIO). They apply to every process of the organisation. After a change, sign in again so your screen picks it up.

06

Good to know

Where the check applies and where it does not.

  • Call and video only. Chat and email have no sound, so they are not checked.
  • Done once per attempt. The result of the check is kept for a few minutes and reused when the transcript is requested, so the file is not analysed twice for one click.
  • Both silence and noise count as “not speech”. The percentage is about speech, not about whether the recording is quiet or loud.
  • Longer recordings take longer. The time to measure grows with the length of the recording.
  • The check is a guard, not a judgement of quality. A recording with a brief but real conversation can still fall below a high minimum; lower the minimum if that is not what you want.
